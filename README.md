## 1. Three checks for every photo

| # | Check | Allowed | Not allowed |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **License** | CC0, Public domain, CC BY, CC BY-SA, Pixabay Content License, Pexels License, Unsplash License (see part 3) | All rights reserved, "Royalty-free" (paid), CC BY-NC, CC BY-ND, Unsplash+, "Sponsored" photos on Pixabay, no license shown, or CC and "©" shown together |
| 2 | **Size** | Background: width ≥ 1920px · Card: width ≥ 640px | Smaller width (the photo becomes blurry when enlarged) |
| 3 | **Author and source** | Author and file page link are known | Author unknown |

> **Important:** "Royalty-free" does **not** mean "free". It means you pay for the license once. Photos from stock sites (Shutterstock, Getty Images, iStock, Alamy) are not used in this exam.

## 2. Where to find the license and size

### Wikimedia Commons
- **Size:** under the photo, "Original file (4,912 × 3,264 pixels …)". The first number is the width.
- **License:** at the bottom of the page, under "Licensing".
- **Author:** in the "Author" row of the table.
- **Download:** the "Download" button → choose a size (e.g. 1920px or 640px). The file is then already optimized.

### Flickr
- **License:** under the photo, on the right: "All rights reserved" or "Some rights reserved". In the second case, click the link and see which CC license it is.

### Stock sites (Shutterstock, Getty Images, iStock, Alamy)
- All photos are paid. **Not used in this exam.**

## 3. Other free sources

| Site | License | Avoid | Author credit |
|---|---|---|---|
| Pixabay (pixabay.com) | Pixabay Content License (CC0 on older photos) | "Sponsored" photos at the top and bottom of search results: these are paid iStock photos | Not required by the license, **required in this exam** |
| Pexels (pexels.com) | Pexels License (CC0 on some photos) | Ads shown on the site | Not required by the license, **required in this exam** |
| Unsplash (unsplash.com) | Unsplash License | Photos marked "Unsplash+": they need a paid subscription. Use the "Free" filter when searching | Not required by the license, **required in this exam** |

**Rules:**
- **Openverse** (openverse.org) searches CC-licensed photos on Wikimedia Commons, Flickr and other collections at once. The license is shown on the photo page.
- Photos from these sites are free, but not without limits. For example, you may not sell a photo unchanged. These limits do not affect an exam web page.
- Avoid photos of recognizable people, logos or brands.

## 5. Record form (fill in README.md, section 6)

Record every photo **you checked**, including **rejected** ones, with the reason.

```markdown
| File in project | Source (link) | Author | License | Size | Used? | Reason |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| vardzia-01.jpg | https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:... | ... | CC BY-SA 4.0 | 3648 × 2736 | Yes (background) | License and size fit |
| - | https://www.alamy.com/... | ... | Paid | - | No | Stock photo, paid license |
| - | https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:... | ... | Public domain | 569 × 388 | No | Too small for a card |
```

On the web page, at the end of each block, write: **Photo - author, source, license.**
Example: *Photo - Kober, Wikimedia Commons, CC BY-SA 4.0.*
